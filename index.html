Why BioBridge?

BioBridge is a browser extension concept designed to reduce password fatigue by connecting biometric authentication with traditional website logins. Instead of asking users to remember and type many passwords, BioBridge aims to support secure login through device-based biometrics and local credential protection.

The Problem

Modern users manage many online accounts, and this often leads to password reuse, weak password habits, and login frustration. Our survey results and background research both suggest that convenience and security are still difficult to balance in everyday authentication.

Research Background

Our literature review focuses on password reuse, usability problems in password-based authentication, the growth of biometrics such as fingerprint and face authentication, and the importance of privacy-by-design in browser-based security tools.

Prototype Direction

We developed a low-fidelity prototype in Figma and a working Chrome extension prototype to simulate the login flow. The prototype includes login detection, biometric verification flow, success states, and a fallback path for unsupported situations.

Research themes

This website is intended to function as a companion transparency page for the BioBridge extension. It explains the reasoning behind the design and summarizes the literature review in a user-friendly way.

Password reuse

Reusing passwords across accounts increases risk when one account is compromised.

Biometric convenience

Biometric systems reduce memory burden and can improve the speed of authentication.

Privacy by design

Trust depends on keeping biometric processing local and explaining system behavior clearly.

Survey findings

Early survey responses showed that password reuse and login frustration remain common, while biometric authentication is already familiar to many users.

What this means

  • Password fatigue is a real usability issue.
  • Users are already familiar with biometric login on devices.
  • Trust is not automatic, so transparency matters.
  • A browser extension should explain how it protects data.

Prototype features

The BioBridge concept includes an extension popup, biometric verification flow, secure credential handling, and a fallback option for unsupported devices or failed verification.

Included in the current prototype

  • Biometric-first login concept
  • Local encrypted credential vault
  • Fallback authentication option
  • Trust and transparency through research report
  • Survey-informed design decisions
